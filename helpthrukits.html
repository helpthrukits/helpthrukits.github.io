Our Goal

Our overall mission is to ease the tensions daily life provides for the low-income populace of the Appleton community to stimulate growth. We strive to create lasting change by supporting communities, empowering individuals, and working toward a brighter future. The first issue we are starting to notice is mishygeine among the homeless population of Appleton. We want to help fix this issue by arranging and donating hygeine kits.
